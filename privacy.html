Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-25

This Privacy Policy describes how rudensama ("I", "me", or "my") collects, uses, and shares information when you use my website (rudensama.github.io) and my games, apps, and other software (collectively, the "Services").

I try to collect as little personal information as reasonably possible. By using the Services you agree to the terms described in this policy.

1. Information I collect

1.1 Information you provide

If you contact me by email, I receive the information you choose to include in your message (your email address, name if provided, and the contents). I use it only to respond to your inquiry.

1.2 Information collected automatically

When you use my games or apps, the following may be collected automatically through third-party SDKs:

  • Device information: model, operating system and version, language, country/region, screen size, and similar technical attributes.
  • Identifiers: advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / Apple IDFA, where permitted), instance identifiers, and randomly generated install IDs.
  • Usage and performance data: sessions, features used, time spent, crash logs, and diagnostics.
  • Network information: IP address (used for general region detection, fraud prevention, and ad delivery) and approximate location derived from IP.

This website itself does not set analytics or advertising cookies. Basic server-side request logs may be retained by GitHub Pages as part of normal hosting operations.

1.3 In-app purchases

If a game or app offers in-app purchases, those transactions are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. I do not receive or store your payment card details. I may receive a transaction identifier and the product purchased so I can deliver the item and provide receipts or refund support.

2. How I use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Services.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve stability and performance.
  • To understand how features are used so I can design better products.
  • To serve and measure advertising (where ads are present), including limiting how often the same ad is shown.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security issues.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Third-party services

I use third-party SDKs and services to run, analyze, and monetize my games and apps. Depending on the specific product, these may include:

  • Analytics & crash reporting: Google Firebase / Firebase Crashlytics, Unity Analytics, GameAnalytics, or similar.
  • Advertising: Google AdMob, Unity Ads, IronSource, AppLovin, Meta Audience Network, or similar.
  • Platform services: Google Play Services and Apple App Store services.

Each provider has its own privacy policy. The data-safety section of each store listing identifies the specific providers used in that product.

  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • IronSource / Unity LevelPlay: is.com/privacy-policy
  • AppLovin: applovin.com/privacy
  • Apple App Store: apple.com/legal/privacy

4. Advertising and tracking

Some products are supported by advertising. Ad networks may use advertising identifiers and limited device information to serve more relevant ads (personalized) or, where you haven't consented or your platform doesn't allow it, non-personalized contextual ads.

You can control ad personalization at the OS level:

  • iOS / iPadOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and → Apple Advertising.
  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can reset or delete your advertising ID.

Where required by law (e.g. in the EEA and UK under GDPR), products will show a consent prompt before any personalized advertising or non-essential tracking.

5. Children's privacy

My products are intended for users aged 13 and older. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact me at the email below and I'll take reasonable steps to delete it.

For users in the EEA, UK, and similar regions, the minimum age may be higher; the same policy applies.

6. Data sharing

I do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only:

  • With the third-party SDKs and platforms described in Section 3, to operate the Services.
  • With Apple and Google for app distribution, payments, and policy compliance.
  • If required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, or property.
  • In connection with a sale of assets or similar transaction, in which case I will provide notice where required.

7. International transfers

The Services use third-party providers whose servers may be located in countries other than your own, including the United States. By using the Services you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in those locations, subject to applicable legal safeguards.

8. Data retention

I retain information only as long as needed for the purposes described here, to comply with legal obligations, to resolve disputes, and to enforce agreements. Analytics, advertising, and crash data are retained according to the relevant providers' retention periods.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • Access, correct, or delete information held about you.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.
  • Receive your data in a portable format.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact me at the email below. I may need to verify your identity first.

10. Security

I use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and I cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes

I may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will update the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, include additional notice inside the affected product.

12. Data Deletion Request

You can request deletion of personal information associated with your use of my games and apps at any time.

Because my apps generally do not require an account or login, the personal data I hold directly is minimal. However, third-party ad and analytics SDKs integrated into my products may collect advertising identifiers and other device-level data as described in Section 3. On Android these typically include Google AdMob, AppLovin MAX, and mediated ad networks routed through those platforms.

To submit a deletion request, email rudensama.dev@gmail.com with the subject line Data deletion request and include:

  • The name of the game or app the request relates to.
  • Your device's advertising ID. To find it: on Android open Settings → Privacy → Ads; on iOS open Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • The platform (Google Play, Apple App Store, or other) and, if you remember it, the approximate date of last use.

I will acknowledge your request and act on it (or explain why I cannot) within 30 days of receipt. Where data is held by my third-party SDK partners — such as Google AdMob, AppLovin, or mediation networks — I will forward your deletion request to the relevant providers so they can remove or anonymize associated records in accordance with their own policies.

Some information may be retained where required by law, for fraud prevention, or in aggregated/anonymized form that no longer identifies you.
