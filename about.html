about me

rudensama

I'm Sod-Erdene Byambatogtokh, a game developer from Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia.

I've made games professionally since 2022: four years at Noodel Games building Make me Float in Unreal Engine, and now Unity mobile games at Black Candy. In my own time I build games in Godot and shipped HexaPress on Google Play.

I like gameplay systems, online multiplayer and fast iteration. I work with AI coding agents every day: I own the design, architecture and tuning, and the agent helps me move faster.

I'm open to remote work. For work, support or anything else, reach out via the contact page.
