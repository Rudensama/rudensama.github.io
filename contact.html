support guidelines
Troubleshooting & Bug Reports

If you are experiencing issues with any of my games or apps, please include the following details in your email so I can assist you faster:

  • App title and current version number
  • Device model and operating system version (e.g., Pixel 7, Android 14)
  • Detailed description of the issue and steps to reproduce it
  • Screenshots or a link to a screen recording, if possible

I review support tickets weekly and aim to reply within 2-3 business days.
